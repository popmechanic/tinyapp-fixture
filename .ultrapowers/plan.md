# The exam derived from a recorded session — the store's history as DoltLite commits, one transition promoted to a green exam with a generated mutant

**Grammar:** claims-v1

**Claim:** I can see a session's store history on the fixture as commits, pick one transition as an exam, and the exam it derives runs green with its mutant killed, without anyone writing the expected state by hand. (elicited)
**Summary:** This records what a TinyApp's store does during a session as a series of commits and turns any one step of that record into a state exam. It exists because two of the five fixture plans on 2026-09-14 parked on an expected state an author wrote from memory, and a state read back from a recording cannot be misremembered. After the run you can list a session's steps, pick one, and get its seed, its expected state, its exam and its mutant written for you, green under the fixture's own suite.

**Goal:** A workspace package `packages/tinyapp-history` beside `tinyapp-exam` and `tinyapp-lint`: the store's content written to a DoltLite file as one commit per callback that moved it, recorded under the exam contract; a session's transitions listed as candidate exams (seed commit, the call, expected commit); the mutant generated by reversing the diff between the two commits; and one transition of the fixture's recorded session promoted under `tests/state-exams/` as a callback exam that runs green with its mutant killed. Authorized by popmechanic/ultrapowers#998 (the map's third first-wave ticket, "Prototype — the exam derived from a recorded session (DoltLite)") within popmechanic/ultrapowers#764's boundaries: DoltLite is a file the exam opens, never a second truth, and nothing of the app's store changes.

**Tech Stack:** Bun + TypeScript + TinyBase (`tinybase/with-schemas` 9.7, a `MergeableStore` under `TABLES_SCHEMA`, callbacks exported from `client/src/storeData.ts`); `@dolthub/doltlite` 0.50.10 (npm; a `node:sqlite`-shaped `DatabaseSync` over the DoltLite engine, prebuilt for linux-x64 and darwin-arm64); the `tinyapp-exam` state exams under `tests/state-exams/` with seeds and expected states under `state-exams/`; `bun run typecheck`, `bun run lint:state`, `bun test`.
**Exam command:** bun test {paths}

**Spec:** popmechanic/ultrapowers#998 (the map: rules 1–6 and the ticket "Prototype — the exam derived from a recorded session (DoltLite)"); popmechanic/ultrapowers#764 (DoltLite's boundaries (1)–(4)); the state-exams spec `2026-09-09-tinyapp-state-exams` (on the laptop, §3.1–3.4 the exam's four parts and the contract). Readings this plan sets out to produce, recorded in Task 4's exam and read off the run: examiner minutes per exam derived (the promoted exam's three files are written by one command) against written (the fixture's own hand-written exams took one sitting each); and the hollow rate of derived mutants — measured on the prototype 2026-09-15 (n=7 transitions, one session, laptop): 6 of 7 transitions promotable, 0 of those 6 hollow, 1 of 7 unpromotable because `tinyapp-exam`'s mutant vocabulary names cells of tables only and that transition changed a store value alone. **Parallelization rationale:** Two waves. Wave 1 is three wide — Task 1 the history writer (the package and its DoltLite file), Task 2 the transition lister, Task 3 the mutant from a diff — each a contract of its own over the shared literals in every Context (the `DiffRow` row shape, the `LogEntry` shape, the commit-message spelling), so none waits on another's code: Task 2 lists off any object with a `log()` and a `diff()`, Task 3 maps diff rows to edits. M3. A recorded call that leaves the store's content as it was makes no commit: `addTodo("")` — refused by the app — resolves to `null` and adds no log entry.

M5. `history.diff(from, to)` is one row per changed cell, in `DiffRow` shape — the columns of `dolt_diff_cells(from, to)` — and a changed store value is one such row with `$values` as its table and `''` as its row; between M2's two commits it is exactly one row: `diff_type` `'modified'`, `to_tbl` `'todos'`, `to_row` `'0'`, `to_cell` `'completed'`, `from_value` `'false'`, `to_value` `'true'`; between the second commit and the `setFilter("done")` commit it is exactly one row: `diff_type` `'added'`, `to_tbl` `'$values'`, `to_row` `''`, `to_cell` `'filter'`, `to_value` `'"done"'`.
M6. A recorded call runs under the exam contract: inside a recorded callback `Date.now()` is `Date.parse(clock)`, and a recorded callback that calls `fetch('http://example.invalid/')` makes the recorded call reject with a message starting `contract breach: http://example.invalid/`.
M7. Recording changes nothing about where the store goes: the state exam that records `addTodo("buy milk")` from `state-exams/seeds/empty.json` reaches `state-exams/expected/one-open-todo.json` and its mutant is killed.

### Task 2: The transition lister — a session's steps as candidate exams, numbered for the pick

**Claim:** I can see a session's transitions listed as candidate exams — each one a seed commit, the call that moved it and the commit it reached — numbered so I can pick one, and the call a line names is one the app can perform. (derived)
Machine: M1. `parseMessage(message)` reads a recorded commit message back into its call: `parseMessage('setTodoCompleted("0", true)')` is `{name: 'setTodoCompleted', args: ['0', true]}`, `parseMessage('clearCompleted()')` is `{name: 'clearCompleted', args: []}`, and each of `parseMessage('seed')`, `parseMessage('Initialize data repository')` and `parseMessage('f([1])')` is `null`.
M2. `listTransitions(reader)` walks `reader.log()` oldest first and yields one `Transition` per entry whose message parses as a call, oldest first: `{seed: <the previous entry's hash>, action: <the parsed call>, expected: <this entry's hash>, message: <this entry's message>, changes: reader.diff(seed, expected)}`; over a reader whose `log()` is, newest first, `c2` `setTodoCompleted("0", true)`, `c1` `seed`, `c0` `Initialize data repository`, it yields exactly one transition with `seed` `'c1'`, `expected` `'c2'`, `action` `{name: 'setTodoCompleted', args: ['0', true]}` and `changes` equal to what `reader.diff('c1', 'c2')` returns; over a log with two calls — `c3` `setFilter("done")` above the three — it yields exactly two, the second with `seed` `'c2'` and `expected` `'c3'`.
M3. `renderTransitions(transitions)` is one line per transition, numbered from 1, joined by `\n`: `<n>. <seed's first 8 characters> --<message>--> <expected's first 8 characters> (<k> change)` with `changes` when `k` is not 1, where `k` is the number of rows in the transition's `changes` — a changed store value is a row like any other; over M2's one transition it is exactly `1. c1 --setTodoCompleted("0", true)--> c2 (1 change)`, and over M2's two it is that line then `2. c2 --setFilter("done")--> c3 (1 change)` when `reader.diff` answers one row for each pair.
M4.
