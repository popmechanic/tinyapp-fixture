# The persistence exam — an unseeded page acts, reloads, and its store and its own SQLite rows equal the expected state

**Grammar:** claims-v1

**Claim:** A **persistence exam**: the helper opens the entry page *unseeded* with the synchronizer stubbed (no `WsSynchronizer`, no network — the page's `WebSocket` is blocked as in #834 and the app's sync branch must tolerate that), performs the action, waits for the persister's save, reloads the page in the same browser profile, and asserts (1) the store after reload equals `expected`, and (2) a `SELECT` through the page's own db handle (the app exposes it beside `window.__TINYAPP_STORE__` when a `window.__TINYAPP_EXAM__` flag is set) returns the expected rows. (quoted from #995)
**Summary:** This adds a second kind of exam to the fixture: instead of starting the page from a planted state, it opens the app exactly as a person would, does one thing, reloads the page, and checks that the thing is still there — in what the page shows, in its store, and in the SQLite rows the page keeps for itself. It exists because until now nothing proved that what you add survives coming back: every exam ran on a page whose saving was switched off by design. After this run two such exams stand on the fixture — a todo added survives a reload, a todo ticked survives a reload — and each leaves the time its save and its reload took on the record, as a reading and not as a pass mark.

**Goal:** The persistence exam of popmechanic/ultrapowers#995 (map #525, the second exam species after the click of #834) on popmechanic/tinyapp-fixture, in three contracts: the app honours a `window.__TINYAPP_EXAM__` flag on an unseeded page — it hands the exam its store, its SQLite handle and its persister and starts no synchronizer; the `tinyapp-exam` helper gains the persistence move — the entry served unseeded from a loopback origin, acted on, saved, reloaded in the same profile, its store and its rows read back, with `persist_ms` and `reload_ms` on `walls.json` and `rows.json` beside the two DOM snapshots; and two exams on the fixture, a todo added and a todo completed, each surviving the reload. The issue's reading is pre-registered there — the reload wall, rows equal to store content on every green exam, the mutant killed — and this plan reports the walls and asserts no bound on them.

**Tech Stack:** Bun 1.4 + TypeScript 6 + TinyBase 9.7 (`tinybase/with-schemas`, a `MergeableStore` under `TABLES_SCHEMA`, `createSqliteWasmPersister` over `@sqlite.org/sqlite-wasm` 3.53 on the `kvvfs` backend — `file:local?vfs=kvvfs`, pages in `localStorage`), React 19 on Vite 8, Tailwind v4 + shadcn/ui; the `tinyapp-exam` state exams under `tests/state-exams/` over raw CDP against `/headless-shell/headless-shell` on the fleet image (`TINYAPP_BROWSER` on a laptop), seeds and expected states under `state-exams/`; `bun run typecheck`, `bun run lint:state`, `bun run lint:ui`, `bun test`.
